RAKTBEEJ ASSETS
===============
Put your own files here. Keep the SAME NAMES and the .mp3 format; the game picks them up automatically.
If a file is missing, effects fall back to a built-in synthesised sound and music is skipped.

assets/music/   (looping tracks, 30-60 s loops are ideal, ~64-96 kbps mono/stereo keeps the game light)
  menu.mp3      main menu, round-end screens
  battle.mp3    during the fight
  boss.mp3      plays when the boss enters his final rage phase (last third of his health)
  (The three loops now are original generated tracks in a Phrygian-dominant scale; replace them with your own if you like.)

assets/sfx/     (short effects, under ~1.5 s each)
  swing.mp3 hit.mp3 hit_heavy.mp3   sword swing, normal hit, big finisher hit
  collect.mp3 combo.mp3             catching blood (pitch rises with the chain), multiplier up
  clone.mp3                         a blood drop lands and a new warrior rises
  hurt.mp3 kill.mp3                 player damaged, warrior slain
  dash.mp3 fury.mp3                 dash, Fury special
  pickup.mp3 shield.mp3             power-ups, shield / dodge
  roar.mp3 slam.mp3 arrow.mp3       boss roar, boss ground slam, archer shot
  win.mp3 lose.mp3 levelup.mp3 ui.mp3   round won, you fell, trophy/upgrade, button click

YouTube Playables notes: only use music and sounds you own or have licensed.
Total bundle should stay small (initial load under 15 MiB, every file under 512 KiB where possible).
